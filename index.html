Who We Are

A Pioneering Force
in Chrome Processing

Our Operations

Upgraded for
Greater Scale

The Rustenburg Wash Plant has recently been upgraded with additional spiral separator capacity, expanding throughput to 80,000 tonnes per month.

Operating since 2018, our facility processes chrome sweepings and tailings through a multi-stage gravity separation circuit, delivering consistently high-grade concentrate.

About VK Chrome

Our Story

Pioneering Chrome
Processing in SA

Founded in 2017, VK Chrome stands as a pioneering force in the realm of chrome processing. With its headquarters strategically situated in Gauteng and its state-of-the-art operational facility located in Rustenburg, Northwest South Africa, VK Chrome has firmly established itself as a key player in the industry.

What sets VK Chrome apart is not only its technological prowess but also its unique ownership structure. As a 100% black-owned enterprise, VK Chrome represents a remarkable triumph in business diversity — spearheaded by the visionary corporate luminary Vuyisile Kona.

At VK Chrome, precision and quality are paramount. The company's cutting-edge processing techniques ensure the extraction of chrome in an environmentally conscious manner while maximising resource efficiency.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Envisioning the Future.
Leading Chrome Excellence.

At VK Chrome, we cast our gaze towards a future where our position as a preeminent chrome processing enterprise is firmly established on the global stage. Our vision transcends boundaries — shaping a legacy that combines innovation, empowerment, and sustainable progress.

South Africa's rich mining heritage is at the core of our identity. We envision our operations as a shining example of the harmonious coexistence between industrial progress and the preservation of natural resources.

Our Mission

Precision. Sustainability.
Empowerment.

Our mission is to propel the frontiers of excellence in chrome processing, while fostering economic empowerment and sustainable growth within our local communities. Our commitment is rooted in a dual purpose: to deliver superior quality chrome products to global markets and to drive socio-economic upliftment through responsible practices.

By nurturing local talent, championing diversity, and providing equitable opportunities, we contribute to the enrichment of our workforce and the sustainable advancement of our nation.

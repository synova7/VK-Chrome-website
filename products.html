What We Supply

Premium Chrome
Concentrate

VK Chrome presents a comprehensive suite of products and services tailored to the dynamic chrome processing landscape. Our chrome concentrate is meticulously processed to serve as a critical ingredient in the production of Ferro chrome — the key alloy in stainless steel manufacturing.

All product is available on a per-tonne basis. Consistent quality, reliable volume, and competitive pricing are the pillars of our supply proposition.

Tested & Certified

Processing Services

Tolling & Washing Services

Beyond product sales, VK Chrome provides processing services for third-party chrome material at a competitive fee per tonne washed.

Chrome Sweeping Service

We accept chrome sweepings from mine floors and processing facilities, running them through our wash circuit for a competitive fee per tonne washed. An efficient solution for mines seeking to recover value from accumulated chrome fines.

  • Charged per tonne washed
  • Supports waste management and resource recovery
  • Available to qualifying mining operations

Tailings Washing Service

We process chrome tailings — material previously processed but still containing recoverable chrome value. Advanced gravity separation technology maximises chrome recovery from historically discarded material.
